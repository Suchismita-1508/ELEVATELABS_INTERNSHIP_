
# 📊 TASK_2 – Data Visualization & Storytelling

## 🎯 Objective
The objective of this task was to create an interactive Power BI dashboard that presents sales data through meaningful visualizations and highlights key business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Superstore Sales Dataset (CSV)

## 📂 Dataset
- Sample - Superstore.csv

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations
- Sales by Category (Bar Chart)
- Sales by Region (Pie Chart)
- Sales by Sub-Category (Treemap)
- Sales Trend by Order Date (Line Chart)
- Sales by Segment and Category (Stacked Bar Chart)
- Profit, Sales, and Discount Analysis (Column Chart)

## 💡 Key Insights
- Technology generated the highest overall sales.
- The West region recorded the highest revenue.
- Phones and Chairs were among the top-selling sub-categories.
- Consumer and Corporate segments contributed significantly to sales and profit.
- Sales trends revealed periods of peak business performance.
